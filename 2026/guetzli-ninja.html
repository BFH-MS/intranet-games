Cookie Ninja

Hold the mouse button (or your finger) and swipe through the cookies to slice them. You have 60 seconds, and combos add time. Leave burnt cookies and coal alone, or you lose a heart!

Cookies sliced in a row raise your multiplier up to ×4; a dropped cookie lowers it. 3+ in one swipe is a combo. Gold starts a cookie frenzy, the ice cookie stops the clock, the giant cookie needs two cuts.

All baked!
